Session 47: The Fall of Thornkeep

📅 January 15, 2026 Session Recap

The party finally confronted Lord Malachar in the throne room of Thornkeep. After an intense battle that saw both victories and devastating losses, the dark lord's plans were revealed - the Crimson Hand had been gathering artifacts of power across the realm, and Thornkeep was merely the first domino to fall.

With Valdris gravely wounded and the city in chaos, the party must now decide: pursue Malachar to his stronghold in the Shadowpeak Mountains, or stay to help rebuild what was lost?

Session Highlights:

  • Epic boss battle with Lord Malachar (Phase 1 complete)
  • Revelation of the Crimson Hand's master plan
  • Valdris critically injured protecting the party
  • Discovery of the Shard of Eternity among the ruins
  • Moral dilemma: Pursuit vs. Protection

Rewards Earned: 8,500 XP each, Shard of Eternity (artifact), +2 Standing with Thornkeep Refugees
